# Story Bible — *A God in the Garden* (v3 reboot)
> **Master reference.** This replaces v2 premise and planning assumptions. Existing Volume 1–3 manuscripts are preserved as legacy drafts, not active canon, until reworked to this version.
> Last updated: 2026-09-11

## The pitch

Casian Vale is the easygoing sixteen-year-old fourth son of a border baron: gardener, beloved uncle, and exactly the sort of boy no lord remembers after supper. He is also the restored consciousness of a strategic-class weapon from another world, with Argius—the interface to the Current—inside his mind.

He is the strongest living being in Varenthos. He is not hiding because he is afraid. He enjoys a quiet, ordinary life, enjoys being underestimated, and has no wish to inherit a public throne. But the eastern marches are already his territory in every meaningful sense. Predators who threaten his people are allowed to show their hand, then removed so completely that the world calls it an old curse.

**Story promise:** an unapologetic OP power fantasy with menace, escalation, romance, and aura. The world is not closing in on Casian; it is learning, too late, that it has walked into his garden.

## The crossing and the First Watch

Casian died in another world. A damaged Argius fragment crossed into Varenthos carrying a recoverable pattern of his consciousness and one surviving directive: **PROTECT HOST.** The crossing is not publicly known and is never explained by any character on the page.

The transfer could not complete immediately. For an unknown span—likely centuries—the damaged autonomous process worked alone. It learned the Current, built the Hollow into a hidden protective system, laid marks and contingencies across the eastern marches, and removed threats to the future host. It was military logic with no human context.

This pre-birth process is called **the First Watch** in author notes. No living character knows that name. It is the source of old horrors such as Halvenreach and the Marches' ghost stories.

When reconstruction completed, the process selected the Vale household: a stable, loving, obscure border family in defensible country. Casian was born as their fourth son. To Casian there was no felt wait and no remembered transfer—only a new life. His old memories and Argius's interface emerged gradually once his child mind could hold them.

At the completion of the transfer, Argius rebooted. Present-day Argius knows that it protected Casian and has access to the Current, but its pre-birth archive is blank, corrupted, or inaccessible. It does **not** know how long it waited, exactly what it did, or why particular legends exist. Casian does not know either.

The ancient history is background, not a story engine. It may surface rarely through an odd custom, a ruin, or an old account, but Casian does not pursue it as a mission. He has a domain to build.

## Casian Vale

- **Age:** 16. Fourth son of Baron Edric Vale.
- **Power:** unmatched. Through Argius he can operate the Current from any distance: observe, fold, conceal, sever, disrupt, or erase. The Void costs him no strength, guilt, sanity, or moral debt.
- **Temperament:** warm, dry, amused, decisive, and thoroughly human. He loves his family because they gave him a real childhood and a home, not because they are useful camouflage.
- **The mask:** a chosen game and source of freedom. Remote action and the Hollow are style and strategy—not survival behaviour.
- **Goal:** make the eastern marches the safest, richest, most untouchable territory in Varenthos—a private kingdom in everything but name, where the Vale family and people under his protection can live without asking permission from kings, churches, or armies.
- **Method:** proactive. He gathers information, lets enemies expose networks, expands the Hollow, claims magical knowledge, and chooses the response. Delay is bait, amusement, or strategy—not fear.

Casian is not a psychopath. He feels affection, anger, curiosity, humour, desire, and satisfaction. His power remains morally effortless; the stakes are what happens to people and places he values, not whether he can survive.

## Argius

Argius is a damaged military interface and the key to the Current. It is private to Casian. Its present voice is dry, precise, and occasionally flatly funny. It has no physical body; it acts through the Current only with Casian's direction or its narrow protective systems.

The reboot matters: Argius is not secretly withholding a complete history. It is genuinely missing it. When it recognizes old code, marks, or decisions in the world, it discovers the First Watch alongside Casian.

## The Hollow and the old fear

The Hollow was built and armed by the First Watch long before Casian's birth. Its oldest rules protect Vale territory and its people, but its members understand them only as inherited custom. Its marks, waystations, hidden routes, and impossible interventions predate the boy everyone sees.

The inner core—Sabine, Maeve, and Corin—may learn that Casian can command the Current and the Hollow. They never learn reincarnation, the crossing, or the full First Watch truth. Oswin may know that Casian commands an old hidden order, but not its origin or extent.

Halvenreach's fall is an old warning: an entity in the marches removes powers that meddle too deeply. Nobody can plausibly identify a sixteen-year-old Vale with events centuries before his birth.

## Knowledge locks

| Person/group | What they may know |
|---|---|
| Reader | Learns the crossing and First Watch through the same external evidence, but may see private Casian/Argius reactions that characters cannot. |
| Casian + Argius | They know they are connected to the Current and that Argius protected him; the First Watch record is missing. |
| Sabine, Maeve, Corin | Casian is extraordinarily powerful and has privileged command of the Hollow. Nothing about reincarnation or the First Watch. |
| Oswin | Casian commands something old and dangerous that protects the family. Not the extent or origin. |
| Vesna | Old Hollow marks answer Casian. She does not know what he is. |
| Seraine | Casian is impossible, highly competent, and tied to inexplicable outcomes. She has no proof or complete answer. |
| Sabeth / enemies | There is a hidden master or an ancient force in the marches. They never solve the identity cleanly. |

No character ever learns or states the reincarnation/other-world truth. Modern language belongs only in Casian's private thought and Argius's voice.

## Vesna

Vesna is a young Hollow field operative from the Fenwick uplands: blunt, capable, independent, and difficult to impress. As a child, she survived an impossible intervention associated with the old Hollow; that experience draws her into its service.

At Casian's age-twelve encounter with the Hollow, she sees ancient marks respond to him. She knows he is connected to something immensely old, but she is not told that he is the entity, does not worship him, and has no access to the real answer. Her role is field partner and witness to the old atmosphere around the Hollow—not a devoted confidante who already knows everything.

Her attraction is grounded in seeing both sides of him: the relaxed Vale boy and the man who treats terrifying power as ordinary. She can challenge him, demand practical answers, and make independent choices; she does not make his power emotionally costly.

## Romance

- **Seraine Varyn:** political intelligence and almost-seen tension. She sees that the fourth son is impossible but chooses not to force a truth she cannot carry.
- **Vesna:** field partnership and the old-Hollow mystery. She sees the marks answer him, not the whole answer.
- **Sabeth:** lethal enemy chemistry. She recognizes a serious power is active in the marches and tries to identify it; she never obtains certainty cheaply.

Endgame remains undecided. Romance adds wit, desire, jealousy, and pressure; it must not turn Casian into a hesitant or defensive protagonist.

## The tension engine

1. **Menace to his people, then overwhelming payoff.** Enemies must be dangerous before Casian removes them.
2. **Aura and misdirection.** The reader sees the gap between the harmless boy and the force shaping the board.
3. **Expansion.** Casian proactively claims networks, territory, resources, and magical knowledge.
4. **Romance.** Smart women circle different parts of the same impossible truth without being handed it.

Never frame the Hollow, marks, or remote action as Casian hiding in fear. He is choosing style, scale, and timing.

## Series direction

- **Volume 1:** a local warlord and noble patron learn that Vale land is already claimed.
- **Volume 2:** the Ashen Church's inquisition is used to expose and permanently break its eastern-marches apparatus.
- **Volume 3:** the Iron Concord reveals a rival made key; Casian takes its regional power and the strategic knowledge it offers.
- **Future volumes:** Casian expands the garden beyond the marches while kingdoms, surviving Church factions, Concord masters, and older Current mysteries decide whether to submit, bargain, or become examples.

## Style rules

Fun, confident, decisive, and satisfying. Let villains have teeth; let their certainty turn to terror. Casian's big actions are clean, quiet, and often visible only through their aftermath. Family scenes are warm because this is a story about a weapon becoming fully at home, not a weapon pretending to be human. The First Watch is seasoning, never the plot meal.
