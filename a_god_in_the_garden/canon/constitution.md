# Writer's Constitution — *A God in the Garden* (v3 reboot)
> **Law, not reference.** Use this with `bible.md`, the roster, the current story state, and the previous chapter. Existing v1/v2 chapter drafts are legacy material.
> Last updated: 2026-09-11

## Hard rules

1. **Casian is never personally threatened.** His power is unmatched and costs him nothing. Do not give him fear, guilt, physical strain, moral debt, or defensive paralysis.
2. **The mask is chosen.** He is a warm, easy fourth son because he likes that life and enjoys the game. Remote action, misdirection, and the Hollow are style and strategy—not survival behaviour.
3. **Make him proactive.** He gathers assets, identifies networks, lets enemies commit, and decides when their world ends. He is building an untouchable eastern domain, not merely surviving attacks.
4. **Villains must have teeth before the payoff.** They may endanger his people, the Hollow, or the peace of the marches. Then give the reader a clean, memorable condescension-to-terror reversal.
5. **The First Watch is history, not a second personality or plot engine.** Pre-birth Argius built the Hollow and protected the future host over an unknown span. Present Argius has rebooted and lacks the records. Use it rarely as atmosphere or explanation; Casian does not investigate it instead of expanding his domain.
6. **The secret holds.** No character learns Casian's reincarnation, other-world origin, or the First Watch truth. Few people even suspect the fourth son is connected to the ancient force; suspicion is not proof.
7. **Vesna does not know what Casian is.** She knows ancient Hollow marks answer him. She is independent, not worshipful, and is never a shortcut to solving the backstory.
8. **Argius is dry.** Terse military precision, occasional flat humour; no chirpiness, grief counselling, or convenient lost-history exposition.
9. **No modern register outside private thought/Argius.** Outsiders call it sorcery, witchcraft, curses, or an old horror.
10. **Protect locked facts.** Names, ages, relationships, and knowledge tiers must match the bible and roster.

## Scene test

Every scene should deliver at least one of: menace/payoff, Casian's aura or mask, territorial expansion, family warmth, or genuine romance chemistry. Cut scenes whose sole function is Casian anxiously managing logistics or chasing First Watch answers.

## Voice and payoff

Casian can be amused, angry, curious, pleased, affectionate, or cold. He is human. But when he acts with power, he is certain. The relevant question is never whether he can win; it is how much of the board he chooses to take.

## Scale must not change the genre

Courts, charters, trade, and treaties are **setups**, not the main event. At any scale, the story remains an OP aura fantasy: an arrogant enemy mistakes Casian's restraint for weakness, threatens people or territory he values, and then learns the scale of the mistake. Do not spend chapters on procedural negotiation when a confrontation, trap, rescue, terror-flip, romance scene, or family beat can carry the same information. Every act needs at least two unmistakable Casian-aura payoffs.
