# Series Arc — v3 reboot scaffold
> **Planning scaffold, not a chapter outline.** Build detailed volume maps only after this direction is approved.

## Series movement

The series starts with an ancient territorial horror that the world mistakes for a buried legend. Casian is born into the centre of it, wakes with unmatched power, and turns the inherited sanctuary into a chosen domain. Each volume makes the garden larger, more visible in consequence, and harder for the world to ignore—without cheaply exposing the fourth son. Court and trade conflicts are stages for menace, traps, and terror-flips, never a replacement for the power-fantasy engine. The First Watch explains the legend; it is not the series quest.

## Volume 1 — The Gardener

**Job:** establish Casian's chosen mask, warmth of the Vale household, the Hollow's old fear, and his proactive claim on the eastern marches. A warlord and patron try to profit from Vale weakness; Casian lets their network surface, then removes them so the border learns it was never unclaimed.

**Background texture:** the Hollow's old rules and Marches legend feel older than Casian, without turning into an investigation.

## Volume 2 — The Ashfire

**Job:** turn an Ashen Church inquisition from a threat into an opportunity. Casian identifies its corrupt eastern apparatus, protects the vulnerable, breaks its regional authority, and establishes the marches as ground the Church will not enter.

**Background texture:** the Church's fear of the Marches legend helps its retreat feel inevitable.

## Volume 3 — The Iron Key

**Job:** introduce the Iron Concord's made key and give Casian a rival magical system worth taking apart. He dismantles its regional network and acquires strategic Current knowledge for his expanding domain.

**Background texture:** the made key confirms that Casian is not the only person able to force the Current, making it a strategic prize rather than a history lesson.

## Non-negotiables

- Casian is proactive, relaxed, and never fear-coded.
- His identity remains hard to prove; the world responds chiefly to effects and the Hollow's legend.
- The First Watch remains rare background texture, never a convenient memory restoration or the series mission.
- Vesna knows the old marks react to him, not the complete truth.
- Family life stays central because it is Casian's chosen purpose, not a disguise he is afraid to lose.
