## Locked premise

- Casian Vale, 16, is an unmatched Current-user. Power costs him nothing; he is relaxed, proactive, and enjoys his harmless-fourth-son mask.
- Casian's long-term project is an untouchable, prosperous eastern marches: a private kingdom in everything but name.
- A damaged Argius fragment crossed from Casian's old world with a recoverable consciousness-pattern and the command **PROTECT HOST**.
- Before Casian's birth, the autonomous **First Watch** built the Hollow and protected the future Vale sanctuary for an unknown span. At Casian's birth Argius rebooted; Casian and present Argius lack its records.
- The First Watch explains the old Marches terror but is not an active investigation or series objective. No character learns Casian's reincarnation or the full truth.
- The Hollow is an old territorial system, not a frightened shield Casian hides behind.
- Vesna knows old marks answer Casian; she does not know what he is and is not a devotee.
