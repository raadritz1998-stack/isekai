# Volume 1 — *The Gardener* (v3 active outline)
> **Status:** active planning canon. Draft into `v3_chapters/` only.
> **Purpose:** establish Casian's chosen mask, the old territorial horror around Vale, and his proactive domain-building project. The First Watch is background texture, not an investigation.

## Volume promise

A warlord and his noble patron mistake the eastern marches for weak, neglected land. Casian lets them show every ally, route, debt, and ambition—then makes it clear that the land was already claimed before either man was born.

## End state

Hask Veyr and Baron Osric are gone; their network is absorbed or ruined; Vale gains a strategically useful eastern strip and control of a key trade road. The Marches begin to whisper about an old protector. Seraine has suspicion without proof; Vesna has seen a mark answer Casian; the reader has one hard clue that the force behind the Hollow predates Casian by centuries.
