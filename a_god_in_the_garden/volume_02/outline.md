# Volume 2 — *The Ashfire* (v3 active outline)
> **Status:** active planning canon. Draft only after Volume 1 v3 and its carry-in are complete.
> **Purpose:** let Casian turn a Church inquisition into a decisive territorial and political victory. The Marches legend strengthens the atmosphere but does not become an investigation.

## Volume promise

The Ashen Church comes east believing its writ, Wardens, and public fire can force the old Marches horror into view. Casian does not merely endure the hunt. He uses it to identify every corrupt official, hostile noble, informant, and hidden Church asset in his territory—then breaks the apparatus so thoroughly that the Church itself marks the eastern marches forbidden ground.

## End state

The Church's eastern inquisition is dismantled; its most dangerous Wardens are erased or publicly ruined; vulnerable Marches communities are folded under Vale protection; Duke Varyn accepts the new reality; and the Church's own archive gives Casian leverage over future Church politics. Seraine becomes a deliberate political ally. Vesna proves herself a partner in building the new order. Sabeth sends the Iron Concord toward the Hollow, not Casian.
