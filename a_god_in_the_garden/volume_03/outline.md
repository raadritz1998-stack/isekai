# Volume 3 — *The Iron Key* (v3 active outline)
> **Status:** active planning canon. Draft only after Volume 2 v3 and its carry-in are complete.
> **Purpose:** introduce a rival magical system and give Casian an opponent worthy of strategic interest. The First Watch remains background texture, not a live mystery arc.

## Volume promise

The Iron Concord comes north with a made key and a disciplined field apparatus, expecting to dissect the Hollow's old protections. Casian welcomes the intrusion as the first chance to seize rival Current knowledge. The Concord can hurt the Hollow, but it can never threaten him; its mistake is believing the Marches mystery is a fortress to besiege rather than a territory whose owner has finally become curious.

## End state

Magister Roeth and the Concord's regional apparatus are broken; Casian holds the made key and strategic Current knowledge; Sabeth leaves alive by his deliberate choice but without proof of his identity; and the wider world now has reason to fear the growing Marches domain.
