# Volume 5 — *Beyond the Hills* (v3 active outline)
> **Status:** planning draft; refine before Arena drafting.
> **Mode:** chosen travel adventure, coastal power fantasy, and creation story. Casian goes to the sea because he wants to.

## Volume promise

Casian has a charter, a safe Marches, and a map covered in western trade routes. He decides that he wants a port—not as a reaction to an attack, but because he has never seen the sea and because a domain that ends at the hills is smaller than he now wants.

He travels west with a deliberately awkward party: Vesna out of the uplands, Seraine balancing politics and pleasure, and Sabeth appearing whenever it benefits her. At Greyhaven, Casian finds a city full of ship captains, smugglers, rival merchant houses, a harbor-master who refuses to be bought, and a sea war that becomes interesting only after he has chosen to join it.

## Casian movement

Casian begins as a proprietor: he takes what his territory needs. He ends as a builder of alliances: he discovers that a port, a city, and a sea route are better when people choose to belong than when they merely obey. That is not mercy or weakness; it is a more satisfying kind of ownership.

## End state

Greyhaven voluntarily joins an open coastal alliance with the Eastern Compact. Casian has a sea route, a new circle of allies, and a larger appetite for building. The Western League is broken as a monopoly, but its people and ships are absorbed, freed, or redirected rather than simply erased. Vesna has an independent adventure and a decisive romantic turn with Casian; Seraine and Sabeth both react rather than remaining frozen.

## New drafting names

- **Mira** — Greyhaven's harbor-master; practical, proud, and impossible to purchase.
- **Nemea** — First Factor of the Western League; sees cities, people, and love as assets to price.
- **Voss** — League admiral and privateer commander; a real sea menace who enjoys fire too much.

|---|---|---|
| 1 | I Want a Port / Casian | Casian tells the family he intends to see the western coast and acquire a harbor. They treat it as a very large holiday. |
| 2 | The Traveling Party / Family | Seraine joins for court reasons, Vesna for field work, and Corin to make certain Casian does not call a ship a garden tool. |
| 3 | Sabeth on the Road / Sabeth | Sabeth appears with a warning that Greyhaven is controlled by a merchant league. Casian thanks her and goes anyway. |
| 4 | Beyond the Hills / Vesna | Vesna sees country and customs beyond the Marches for the first time; the travel itself gives her a story not defined by Casian. |
| 5 | The City of Masts / Casian | Casian reaches Greyhaven, sees the sea, and is genuinely delighted. He decides the city is worth having as a friend. |
| 6 | The Harbor-master Says No / Mira | Mira rejects Casian's first friendly offer because she has survived too many men who arrive promising protection. He likes her immediately. |
| 7 | A Race of Sails / Seraine–Casian | A public regatta introduces the coastal factions. Casian wins a ridiculous wager through quiet Current manipulation and becomes the city's newest rumor. |
| 8 | The First Factor's Smile / Nemea | Nemea offers Casian a place inside the League. He declines because the terms are boring. |
| 9 | A Ship on Fire / Voss | Voss burns a refugee ship outside the harbor to frighten Greyhaven into obedience. Real danger arrives after Casian is already invested. |
| 10 | The Wave That Took It / Casian | Casian saves survivors and removes the fire-ship under a sudden wall of fog, giving Greyhaven its first glimpse of the sea's new ghost. |
| 11 | Supper Over the Water / Casian–Seraine | Casian and Seraine talk above the harbor. She sees that he is not acquiring a port to win; he simply wants a wider life. |
| 12 | The Festival of Lamps / Multiple POV | Casian helps Greyhaven host a defiant harbor festival. The League responds by declaring the city commercially closed. |

| 25 | Fire at the Docks / Voss | Voss launches a full fire-ship assault. The docks, workers, and the city Casian chose are genuinely at risk. |

| 28 | The Island Ledger / Maeve–Sabeth | Maeve and Sabeth raid the League's island reserve for blackmail records, coin, and ships; their rivalry becomes reluctant respect. |
| 29 | Nemea's Empty Price / Nemea | Nemea finds every purchasable thing gone: captains have chosen, records are public, and her monopoly has become a joke. |
| 30 | The City Is Not for Sale / Casian–Nemea | Casian offers Nemea the chance to leave with her life and nothing else. She takes it, carrying a story about what the sea now belongs to. |
