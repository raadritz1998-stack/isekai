# Volume 6 — *The Open Gate* (v3 active outline)
> **Status:** planning draft; refine before Arena drafting.
> **Mode:** initiated world-order finale. Casian calls the world to his gate; the final betrayal is an escalation, not the old volume-opening loop.

## Volume promise

Casian returns from the sea and decides the Compact has become too important to let other powers define it through rumor, fear, and private demands. He calls the Open Gate Conclave at Vale: every kingdom, port, Church remnant, and rival power may come to speak, bargain, or reveal what it wants.

The first half is a gathering of impossible personalities inside Casian's home territory: rulers walking into the garden, family meals with diplomats, Seraine in her element, Vesna building security, Sabeth returning by choice, and Casian enjoying the audacity of it all. High Regent Severin turns the Conclave into a trap only when he realizes he cannot control its outcome. His betrayal creates the final grand payoff—but the story begins with Casian choosing the stage.

## Casian movement

Casian begins the series wanting to be left alone with his family. He ends it choosing a visible consequence without becoming publicly exposed: he wants the world beside his garden to be worth having. He does not take a throne. He becomes the unseen author of an order people willingly choose because they have seen the alternative fail.

## End state

The Covenant of Crowns fails at the Open Gate. The resulting Accord recognizes the Eastern Compact and its coastal allies as an independent permanent power, protected by local autonomy rather than Casian's personal rule. Casian remains publicly the fourth son. The world knows a vast force guards the Compact, but no enemy can prove the boy in the garden is that force. The romance endgame resolves after user selection; the other women retain full agency and meaningful futures.

## New drafting names

- **Severin** — High Regent of Lyria; brilliant, controlled, and convinced every power must kneel to an accountable crown.
- **Ivara** — Speaker for the Covenant; gifted diplomat who wants peace but begins by serving the wrong structure.
- **Tarek** — commander of the Covenant's field armies; practical, formidable, and dangerous without being sadistic.
