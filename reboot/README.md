## What's here

- `00_discussion.md` — the diagnosis and the argument that led to the reboot.
- `canon/bible.md` — premise, the Current, Argius, the exile, the Keeping, character and knowledge locks.
- `canon/constitution.md` — the law. Rewritten for the new engine; several rules are new.
- `canon/series_arc.md` — five volumes, each a different *kind* of story.
- `canon/roster.txt` — canonical names, ages, roles. **Generated** — do not edit.
- `canon/ages.md` — age of every character in every volume. **Generated** — do not edit.
- `canon/characters.json` — the single source of truth for names, ages, and the calendar. Edit this.
- `tools/age_ledger.py` — regenerates the two above; `--check` verifies, `--check-year` audits chapters.
