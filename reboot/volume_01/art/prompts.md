# Volume 1 — *The Gardener* · Image prompt sheet

Everything here is written to be pasted straight into an image model. Each prompt is self-contained: it already carries the house style, so you can run them one at a time in any order.

**How to use this sheet**

1. Set your model to **2:3 portrait** for character pieces, **3:2 landscape** for scenes and interiors, **16:9** for the wide world plates.
2. Paste the **Style block** at the top of every prompt — it is already embedded in each one below, so you only need it separately if you want to lock a style reference image.
3. For consistency, generate **Casian first**, pick the one you like, then use it as an **image/style reference** for every other character prompt (keep the note "use the reference only for art style, line work and soft cel-shaded rendering"). Do not use it as a face reference or everyone ends up related.
4. Keep the **same seed** across the eight portraits if your model allows it — it holds the rendering together even when the faces differ.
5. Append the **Negative prompt** to every generation if your tool has a field for it.

---

## Style block (the house look)

> Anime film illustration, cel-shaded with soft hand-painted shading, clean thin line work, muted earthy northern-European palette — moss, slate, wool-grey, oat, faded ochre — flat overcast light with one warm accent per frame, detailed painted background, no gloss, no neon, no bloom, quiet and lived-in, 1990s–2000s anime feature film aesthetic.

**Negative prompt (all images)**

> text, lettering, captions, watermark, signature, runes, glyphs, modern clothing, modern objects, zippers, plastic, telephone poles, cars, guns, glowing magic effects, lens flare, oversaturation, glossy digital painting, western cartoon proportions, chibi, exaggerated expression, melodrama, collage, multiple panels, split screen, grid.

## Character anchors

Reuse the bolded block verbatim inside any prompt where that character appears. This is what keeps Casian the same boy in nine pictures.

**CASIAN VALE — 16.** Lean sixteen-year-old boy, slightly overgrown dark brown hair, grey-green eyes, plain slate-blue wool coat over a coarse linen shirt, dark work trousers, muddy boots, no ring, no badge, no ornament of any kind. Pleasant, calm, unreadable face; stands with his hands in his pockets.

**ELNA VALE — 12.** Twelve-year-old girl, dark brown hair in a braid, brown eyes, brown wool dress under a rough shawl, barefoot or in tied boots, small and quick.

**SERAINЕ VARYN — 19.** *(spell: Seraine)* Nineteen-year-old noblewoman, auburn hair pinned up under travel, green eyes, high-collared grey-green riding coat with a plain clasp, kid gloves, a rolled document never far away. Courteous, composed, keeping her own counsel.

**VESNA ORROW — 19.** Tall, weathered nineteen-year-old upland woman, roughly cut black hair, brown skin, heavy dark-green hooded wool cloak over a work dress, wooden walking staff, small leather book tied at her belt, worn boots.

**HASK VEYR — 40s.** Hard-faced chieftain, iron-grey beard, weathered skin, heavy fur-lined coat, a book of tallies, hands that have done a great deal of work.

**TOLAN — 41.** Lean, tired soldier, greying short beard, cropped hair, ink-stained fingers, plain dark-brown coat over a jerkin, three bound books and a mended saddlebag.

**MAEVE CORRAN — 40.** Miller, sleeves tied back, coarse apron, grey-streaked brown hair escaping a kerchief, flour on her forearms, a leather business book and a pencil.

**EDRIC VALE — 54.** Baron, tired kind face, grey-brown beard kept short, good but plain wool, a lord who looks like a farmer who was told to dress for a hall.

**MAREN VALE — 40s.** Baroness, dark hair braided and pinned, sleeves tied back, no jewellery, an expression that has already decided what you are going to say.

**OSRIC OF THORNWALL — 50s.** Rich, careful, frightened; velvet that is eleven years out of fashion and very good, ringed hands, a face built for courtesy.

**RENNICK — 50.** Factor. Pleasant face doing nothing in particular. Good coat, clean hands, a ledger under his arm.

## 1. World plates

### 1.1 Vale at dawn — the garden and the hill
> Anime film background art, cel-shaded, hand-painted detail, muted earthy northern-European palette, soft overcast dawn light. A small stone manor with slate roofs on a low green hill, a high walled garden beside it, bare rose beds cut back hard for winter, a stretch of stone wall at the east end that plainly needs mending. Hedgerows and wet fields fall away below in layers of mist; one tiny horseman on a distant ridge. Quiet, unremarkable, lived-in. No people in the foreground. — 16:9.

### 1.2 The mill at Ashgrove
> Anime film background art, cel-shaded, painted detail, muted palette, cold February dusk. A stone watermill at the foot of a shallow hill, a large wooden wheel turning in a newly dug millrace, a small bell hanging in a wooden frame on the roof ridge. Wet mud road, a laden cart, lanterns being lit, a walled yard with its gate standing open, sacks stacked under a lean-to. A slate notice nailed to the mill wall, the writing illegible. No text in image. — 3:2.

### 1.3 The cut — eleven gates
> Anime film background art, cel-shaded, painterly, muted moss-green and grey palette, thin morning mist. An old upland road runs along a hillside; a stone-paved ford descends through a steep grassy bank, and beside it a gap in a low stone wall where a wooden gate once hung. Six more crossings recede along the hillside into mist. Far off, three small hooded figures with one lantern. Lonely, old, faintly wrong. — 16:9.

### 1.4 The drove in March
> Anime film background art, cel-shaded, muted palette, wet grey afternoon. The worst road in a county: eleven miles of rutted mud over open common land, a flooded stretch at the fourth mile, no wall, no house, no fold, nothing at the end of it but a dark line of hills. A single plank bridge over a beck. Rain coming across in sheets. — 16:9.

### 1.5 Thornwall — the room with four cups
> Anime scene illustration, cel-shaded, muted palette, cold north light through a narrow window. A small stone room off a great hall: a plain table with four cups and a jug, one cup unused, a banked fire, a chair pulled out and not sat in. Nobody in the room. The door is shut. — 3:2.

## 2. Character portraits

### 2.1 Casian *(generate this one first — it sets the style)*
> Anime character portrait, cel-shaded with soft painted shading, muted palette, overcast light. **CASIAN VALE — 16**, hands in his pockets, standing in front of a high stone garden wall with roses cut back hard, his expression pleasant, calm and completely unreadable. Half-body, quiet and ordinary rather than dramatic. — 2:3.

### 2.2 Elna, behind the stone
> Anime scene illustration, cel-shaded, muted cold palette, February water light. **ELNA VALE — 12** crouches low behind a big wet stone in a shallow millrace, water to her knees, both hands pressed flat to the stone, eyes wide and steady, absolutely silent. A wicker basket lies forgotten in the lane beyond. Reeds, icy water, stone bank, low grey light. Tense and restrained, no violence shown. — 3:2.

### 2.3 Seraine
> Anime character portrait, cel-shaded, muted palette, overcast light. **SERAINЕ VARYN — 19** stands in the courtyard of a great stone hall, chin level, one gloved hand holding a rolled document, courteous and utterly unreadable. Quiet authority rather than glamour. — 2:3.

### 2.4 Vesna
> Anime character portrait, cel-shaded, muted palette, cold dusk. **VESNA ORROW — 19** stands on a moorland road above a cut in the hillside, staff in one hand, looking back over her shoulder with a flat, deliberate, entirely unintimidated expression. Wiry strength, no glamour. — 2:3.

### 2.5 Hask, in the tent
> Anime scene illustration, cel-shaded, single warm lamp against cold night. **HASK VEYR — 40s** sits at a campaign table inside a canvas tent, fur-lined coat across his shoulders, a book of tallies open, one hand flat on the table, two cups beside the lamp and one of them full. The expression of a man doing arithmetic in the dark and not liking the answer. — 3:2.

### 2.6 Tolan
> Anime character portrait, cel-shaded, candlelight. **TOLAN — 41** sits at a rough table writing in a bound book, two more books stacked beside him, looking up from the page with dry, patient intelligence. Inn-room interior, ledgers, a mended saddlebag. — 2:3.

### 2.7 Maeve
> Anime scene illustration, cel-shaded, dusty light through a mill window. **MAEVE CORRAN — 40** stands at a tall sloping desk with a leather-bound business book open and a pencil in her hand, reading a line and not liking it. Behind her, mill stones, sacks, a ladder up to a bell. Dry competence, no glamour. — 2:3.

### 2.8 Edric and Maren *(paired)*
> Anime character portrait, cel-shaded, muted interior light. **EDRIC VALE — 54** and **MAREN VALE — 40s** stand side by side in the hall of a small stone manor: he looks like a farmer who was told to dress for a hall, tired and kind; she has her sleeves tied back and an expression that has already decided what you are going to say. A bowl of roses past their best on the table behind them. — 3:2.

### 2.9 Osric *(the man about to lose a county)*
> Anime character portrait, cel-shaded, cold hall light. **OSRIC OF THORNWALL — 50s** sits alone at the end of a long table with a cup he is not drinking from, velvet eleven years out of fashion and very good, ringed hands folded. Rich, careful, frightened. — 2:3.

## 3. Interactions *(the heart of the volume — run these as scenes, not portraits)*

### 3.1 The council, and the boy with the jug — ch. 21
> Anime scene illustration, cel-shaded, muted palette, firelight and rushlight. Five men sit at a table in a baron's hall deciding what to burn next: a tired lord, a plump factor with a ledger, two soldiers, and a hard-faced chieftain. The room is entirely competent and entirely unremarkable. At the edge of the frame, half in shadow and not part of the table, a **boy of sixteen in a plain coat carries a jug and pours for them and is not looked at twice.** The only person who is watching him is the soldier with the ink-stained fingers. — 3:2.

### 3.2 The bread in the yard — ch. 32
> Anime scene illustration, cel-shaded, muted palette, cold morning light and steam. The walled yard of a stone mill, its gate standing open: a **baron and his family at a table with bread and a barrel of small beer**, a girl of twelve carrying a water jug between two hundred armed men in wet coats with their bows still on their backs, forming an orderly queue. On the wall behind, one man with a bow and no arrow on the string, visibly bored. Nothing is violent; everything is a decision. — 3:2.

### 3.3 The tent — Casian and Hask, ch. 34
> Anime scene illustration, cel-shaded, muted palette, one lamp. Inside a canvas tent at night: **HASK VEYR — 40s** sits across a small table from **CASIAN VALE — 16**, who is sitting on a stool with his hands in his pockets and a full cup in front of him that he has not touched. The lamp between them is the only warm thing in the frame and it is burning steadily. Neither man is armed. Both faces are calm. The scene is a conversation and it is the most frightening image in the volume. — 3:2.

### 3.4 The millrace dug in daylight — ch. 25
> Anime scene illustration, cel-shaded, muted palette, flat March daylight. Forty people stand on the muddy bank of a millrace watching men dig silt out in barrows and lay it along the top of the bank. A woman with a ledger and a boy writing beside her; two men pacing out a line with poles; a man complaining in the foreground and being listened to seriously. A notice is nailed to a church door in the far background, illegible. Ordinary work, done in public. — 3:2.

### 3.5 The cloister at Harven — ch. 37
> Anime scene illustration, cel-shaded, muted palette, grey afternoon light in a stone cloister. **SERAINЕ VARYN — 19** and **CASIAN VALE — 16** stand a few feet apart under the arches, not touching, not close; she has a folded document in both hands, he has his hands in his pockets and is answering plainly. Behind them, a **Royal Marshal in a plain dark cloak stands in the shadow of the third arch, watching** and pretending not to. — 3:2.

### 3.6 The Tuesday at the mill — ch. 38
> Anime scene illustration, cel-shaded, muted palette, dusty interior. A mill floor cleared for a hearing: a miller with a book at a plain table, a man standing up and saying something he regrets, a widow with her arms folded, nine neighbours watching from a bench, a boy of nine in the foreground holding a knife he has just been made to give up and looking furious. No lord, no lawyer, no oath. — 3:2.

### 3.7 The bench in the garden — ch. 1 / ch. 40 *(mirror pair)*
> Anime scene illustration, cel-shaded, muted palette, soft morning light. A low stone bench in a walled garden, roses cut back hard, an old man's coat folded on the arm of it, a girl of twelve sitting with her feet not quite reaching the gravel. Nobody is talking. The wall behind is warm with the first sun of the year. — 3:2.

### 3.8 Three buds, October — ch. 40
> Anime scene illustration, cel-shaded, muted palette, cold clear evening. A young walnut tree newly planted in the corner of a walled garden, mud still fresh around it, a hurdle leaned against it for wind; **three small green buds on the scion, unmistakably out of season.** A boy of sixteen and a girl of twelve stand looking at them. The garden gate behind is shut. — 3:2.

## 4. Optional extras

### 4.1 The fair — reading the articles from a barrel
> Anime scene illustration, cel-shaded, muted palette, June sunlight. A fair in a village street: a **boy of sixteen standing on a barrel reading from four sheets of paper** to four hundred people, his voice clearly not carrying as far as he would like, a man at the back shouting a question, a miller selling loaves, a baron seated at the back of the crowd letting him do it. — 16:9.

### 4.2 Nineteen horses on an open road — ch. 27
> Anime scene illustration, cel-shaded, muted palette, flat grey February afternoon. An empty road at a ford: **nineteen saddled horses standing in a loose bunch with their reins down and their heads up, saddles unbuckled and laid straight, girths folded back over the seat, no rider anywhere.** Two riders on the far shoulder looking back at them. No violence, no blood, nothing to see. — 16:9.

### 4.3 Everything asks for a paper *(the map that isn't a map)*
> Anime scene illustration, cel-shaded, muted palette, lamplight. A tally-room wall covered with a hand-drawn map of a county — roads, fords, folds, eleven small marks along a line of hills — and a man of forty-four with his hat in his hands looking at it while a boy of sixteen stands beside him with his hands in his pockets. Pins, string, a ledger. Illegible markings only. — 3:2.
