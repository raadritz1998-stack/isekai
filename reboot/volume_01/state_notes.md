## Start state

- Casian Vale, 16, fourth son of Baron Edric. The Current, Argius, and a world he remembers and cannot name.
- **No network. No order. No reputation.** Volume 1 is the only volume where he is genuinely alone, and it should feel like it.
- The Keeping is a folk custom everybody practises and nobody explains.
- The Vale barony is small, warm, and quietly prosperous. Edric is a good lord who sees an ordinary son.
- Hask Veyr holds the eastern hills. Osric of Thornwall wants the road the Vales sit on.
- Seraine and Sabeth have not entered the story. Seraine arrives ch. 17, light touch only.

## Standing locks

- Casian is never threatened, never strained, never afraid. His only deaths are ones he did not prevent, and the volume allows exactly one (Aldous, ch. 19).
- Nobody learns the old world. Argius remembers; Argius does not speak of it beyond what is needed.
- The mask is pleasure, not pressure. Ch. 6 and ch. 31 must both carry this.
- No modern register outside Casian's private thought and Argius's voice.
- Vesna is not a romance lane. Seraine is, and in volume 1 it is *politics and sharp company* — she is nineteen and he is sixteen and the volume must not pretend otherwise.
- The Hollow is feared from its first appearance (ch. 7–8) and is described as a curse before it is described as anything else.

## Continuity log

- **ch_001 *The Bench* (Casian).** Morning in the walled garden; roses cut back hard; Elna (12) on the bench. A scout on the ridge, one and a quarter miles out, sent from Hask's country the long way round — Casian turns the horse and the man goes home without ever knowing, from a bench, without standing up. Oswin (71) says the most suspicious thing about him is how pleasant he is. Supper, twelve at the table, Emric (1) beating a spoon. **New:** Edric reveals four riders "going west, not stopping" three days back on the Ashgrove road — the first thread of the ch. 5 burn. Edric's line to hold: *"You're the only one of them that doesn't want something from me. It's restful and it worries me."* After supper Casian walks the ridge and stands where the scout sat, looking down at his own house the way a stranger would. Ends on the walnut and Argius's line: *"You will be here for all of it."*
  - Irreversible: nothing yet, and that is deliberate. No one in the house knows. No name has been spoken aloud that matters.
  - Seeded: the Ashgrove road, four riders, Osric unnamed, Hask unnamed, the walnut, the east wall needing stone before the wet.
- **ch_002 *Forty Years* (Casian).** The graft takes in four days. The water channel and its four leather gates. Maren watches him open the last one and says nothing — *"the flat neutral interest she used for the water channel"* is her whole character. Breakfast. Hob the mason mentions building work at Thornwall paid for out of a purse too full. Gareth reports on Hask Veyr: a hundred men, no title, a slate roof, bought land and kept the sellers — *"he's going to buy a friend first."* Oswin asks what he will do when the next bad man comes. Argius asks what it actually retains: a street that sloped, a smell, seven words with no grammar, a number whose purpose is gone. Ends on the walnut and *"I will."*
  - Irreversible: Argius's archive is confirmed as fragments, not records — there is no history to recover.
  - Seeded: Hask named for the first time. Thornwall money with no obvious source.
- **ch_003 *What the Marches Keep* (Casian).** Fenwick, the moved boundary stone, Reed and Tolsey both honest and both wrong. Casian settles it without a verdict and both men leave believing they won. Calls out that a stone was lifted and set down two or three seasons ago, not walked. The Keeping explained as custom: nobody knows why, the Ash has given up on it, and the fear is real and never discussed. A frightened farmer, oxen that would not pass the Fenwick stone. Calla: *"Don't be charming at me, it's the least interesting thing about you."* He gives her cuttings. Edric's report scene: *"I want you to grow up in a country that isn't frightened of its own fields."*
  - Irreversible: **someone moved a Keeping stone two seasons before the story and nobody saw it.** Open thread, deliberately unexplained.
  - Seeded: Calla now watching him. Edric has decided which questions he can afford.
- **ch_004 *The Ledger at the Mill* (Maeve).** The mill's paper is held by Rennick of Thornwall, 412 marks, 131 of it fraud. Casian reads the town — eleven shops on one street carrying paper from the same factor, and Osric's steward riding a horse bred in Hask's hills. He buys the paper at full price in coin in front of a clerk, on purpose, so that Rennick has money and no story. Offers Maeve a loan at no interest for six years and asks for one thing: the book, and to be told what the numbers mean. Oswin's warning: *"It concerns you. Every time, in the small hours, in a room with no one in it."* Edric notices, and says nothing, and starts watching.
  - **Dependency count: 1 (Maeve).**
  - Irreversible: he has spent most of his capital and has a creditor's claim on a miller, which is the first thing he owns that can be taken from him.
  - Seeded: Osric's steward, the eleven shops, Rennick, Brennan's loan (Brennan will want to know eventually).
- **ch_005 *Ash on the Road* (Family).** Mirra Cott (50) and Jon (19) killed at the Ashgrove holding. Tolan leads, counts the dead himself, and now carries a number that stands at thirty-one since he started counting six years ago. Edric: law is slow and it is what we have. Maren privately tells Casian she has known for twenty years that the letters are worth nothing: *"do it so that I am never sat in a kitchen being told about it by a man with a hat in his hands."* Casian identifies the fifth road — a drove over the Common that carries goods, not men.
  - Irreversible: **Mirra and Jon are dead.** Casian's founding reason is now on the page. Tolan is a named, thinking, reluctant antagonist.
- **ch_006 *The Easy Brother* (Casian).** The Harrowmere fair. Wool now goes east, over the cut, up into the hills. Hask is "polite" and remembers his stable boys' names — Casian dislikes that he sees himself in it. Meets Rennick, who is cheerful and unafraid and warns him off lightly. Confirms the drove road as a fifth route. Writes it down: *anything I know about, I can do — the whole difficulty is the knowing.*
  - Irreversible: Rennick has now seen his face and been pleasant to him.
- **ch_007 *The Fence at Harrowmere* (Casian).** Ansel Trask, 60, the fence of the eastern road, whose paper has been quietly taken from him over two years — he has become a clerk and no one has noticed. Casian tells him so. Eleven names, one of them Osric four years old, one Rennick. Ansel's condition: the truth about what he was. Casian's realisation afterwards: *he let me win.*
  - **Dependency count: 3 (Maeve, Ansel, and a promise to a miller).**
  - Irreversible: eleven names exist in Casian's hand only, and Ansel has burned his own deniability.
- **ch_008 *The Man Who Waits* (Corin).** Corin Ahl, 35 years an armsman, given the first task in his life that uses the whole of him: a wagon down the Ashgrove road as bait, reported to the baron rather than to Casian. Finds a cold fire square on the drove — nine men, ten nights, sited properly. Concludes the enemy has a soldier working for a man who isn't one. Jory covers for him. Edric's line: *"Is he right?"*
  - Irreversible: **the Hollow now has a second working part and its first real risk.** Corin has written a private note asking why a boy of sixteen can see what he cannot.
- **ch_009 *What Osric Wants* (Osric).** The top of the enemy ladder on the page. Osric, 55, careful for thirty years, a genuinely fair landlord who gets down off his horse to settle a boundary. He is not stupid: he works out that paying the fraudulent 131 marks means the boy read the book. He proposes the eastern strip as eleven parcels of nobody's land, and writes to Hask *without naming him*, to the quiet man on the grey horse. Seraine Varyn's inspection is announced. Hask's reply arrives in nine days and offers Osric his own ambition back, and Osric burns it and understands he is being priced.
  - Irreversible: **Osric has committed and Hask has his measure.**
- **ch_010 *The Empty Ford* (Casian–Tolan).** Casian takes out the causeway on the Veyr — not the men, the crossing. He does it from the garden bench with a cup in his hand, and it rains, and the stone goes. Four drivers walk home with a story about a mist. Tolan investigates for two days, drags the channel, interviews all four separately, and works out that a story which does not improve in the telling is a story somebody has thought about. He is laughed at in the hall and writes it down in a book titled *Things that should not have been so.*
  - Aura payoff #2: **a place that isn't there.**
  - Irreversible: Hask has a winter with a hundred men and no grain. **Tolan now has a written record.**
- **ch_011 *The Girl Who Walks in the Dark* (Vesna).** Vesna Orrow, 19, uplander, hired by Corin. Nine runs. On the seventh she hears two men and a horse going north on the moor road — toward a place, not a raid. Then she walks the cut and finds **four gates with no gates in them**, a line of crossings built into an old wall above the line where any road would go, weathered as old as the wall itself. She asks what she is part of and is told the truth. She asks for Fenwick to be told about her, and for it to be said by Casian and not by her.
  - Irreversible: **the four gates are the second unexplained old-country thread** (with the moved Fenwick stone). She is in.
- **ch_012 *The Shape of It* (Casian).** The old dairy. Five people around a map for two hours. Maeve, Corin, Vesna, Ansel and Casian. Money argument: two riders cost twenty marks a quarter, Maeve lends nine, Ansel four, and Casian refuses any more lending because *the day it costs me more than it costs you is the day you're entitled to a vote.* Ansel's point that wins the evening: the danger is not the men coming out of the hills, it is the forty men already in the county who owe somebody outside it. Casian says the thing out loud once — that he has brought a map, a purse and a bad idea, and everything else was built by them. Then: **Hask gets one move**, so every concealed friend has to come into the open.
  - Dependency count: **14**.
  - Irreversible: the thing has a shape and five people know it has a shape. Elna reports a grey-coated man who counted eleven at table in the spring. Edric says he will not ask, and that he will know.
